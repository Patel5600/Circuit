11 · Transaction toasts

A four-step Solana lifecycle, plus rejection toasts. A borrow checks the risk store first, so a gated one is rejected with the reason, no signature needed.

12 · Activity timeline

The line draws as you scroll past it, and each node lights when the line reaches it. Sample data.

(Circuit)Activity
The Risk store is a ~20-line pub/sub: one Risk.set() updates the gate dial, metric cards, borrowing power, ladder, market conditions, topology and agent status together. In a Vite + React app this becomes a zustand store; every component here just reads Risk.get() and calls Risk.on().
