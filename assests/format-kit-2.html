Ink trail

A metaball trail: circles are drawn on a canvas, blurred, then thresholded with a contrast filter so they fuse like liquid. It wanders on its own until you take over.

Draw
with ink

Inertia ribbon

An endless strip of shaped cards. Drag it and it keeps its momentum. Speed tilts the cards, and a sine wave tied to the screen makes the strip ripple as it passes through.

Drag, or use the arrow keys

Scroll morph

The section pins while one shape travels through five formats: circle, pill, vertical, horizontal, then the full frame turns to ink. Scroll position is the only input.

Melting headline

An SVG displacement map that only pushes pixels downward. Its strength follows how close the cursor is, and it is removed completely when idle.

Everything
can melt

Bring your cursor close

Kinetic ring

Two counter-rotating rings of text on SVG paths, each spaced by script to fill its circumference exactly. Scroll speed pushes them; hovering slows them so they can be read.

Format cursor

A cursor that becomes the shape of whatever it touches: word, card, circle or pill. Position, size and corner radius are eased separately, and the shape leans slightly toward the pointer.

Ink lab

A generative poster maker. The sliders write directly into one SVG filter, and the filter markup you tuned is printed below so you can paste it into your own site.

(Format)Format
Shared by several blocks: the SVG filters at the top of the body and the .ink class. The scroll morph and kinetic ring read scroll position, so open the file in its own tab to feel them at full speed. Each block is a self-contained script closure under its own comment header.
