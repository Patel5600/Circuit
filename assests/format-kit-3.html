Preloader

The page-load sequence you just saw. One shape morphs through four formats as a counter climbs, then grows to fill the screen, fills with ink, and rolls away like a curtain. Drive it from real progress, or let it simulate one.

Loader.set(0.42);    // 0 to 1: feed it real progress (fonts, images, data)
Loader.finish();     // grow to full frame, ink, curtain up, unlock scroll
Loader.play(3400);   // or simulate a load that stalls and jumps like a real one

Dial carousel

Twelve cards stand around a circle like the numerals of a clock. Drag to spin; on release the ring snaps to the nearest hour with a soft ease. It idles forward by itself, and you can click a card to bring it to the front.
